# Smokehouse v1.0.0

**Cure meat and dry vegetables without a single unit of power.**

Pemmican is the only travel food a low-tech colony can make, and it needs meat *and* vegetables in the pantry at the same time. Smokehouse adds the missing half of the picture: a wood-fired preservation bench that works on one ingredient at a time.

Perfect for hunting colonies, tribal starts, desert outposts, ice sheet runs and gravships with no room for a cooler.

## Features

- **New research — Smoke preserving.** Neolithic, 600 points, unlocked after Pemmican.
- **New building — Smokehouse.** A 2x1 timber shed around a smouldering fire pit. 55 wood + 10 steel, burns wood, no power, no components. Pushes a little heat while it works, and it can be uninstalled and carried like any workbench.
- **Two new bills.** *Smoke meat* (raw meat only) and *Dry vegetables* (raw plant food only) — no more juggling two food types to make travel rations.
- **Two new foods.** Smoked meat (45 days) and dried vegetables (60 days). Light, stack to 75, and they take up far less caravan mass than the raw ingredients they came from.
- **A small reward.** +2 mood "ate cured provisions" for a day. Smoke, salt and patience taste like a job well done.
- **Chinese Simplified** translation included.

## Numbers

| | In | Out | Rot | Mass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smoked meat | 0.5 nutrition meat | 5 × 0.12 | 45 days | 0.025 |
| Dried vegetables | 0.5 nutrition plants | 6 × 0.10 | 60 days | 0.02 |
| *Pemmican (vanilla)* | 0.25 meat + 0.25 plants | 16 × 0.05 | 70 days | 0.018 |

Cured provisions are slightly less efficient than pemmican. In exchange they need one ingredient instead of two and nothing but firewood to run.

## Installation

1. Download `rimworld-smokehouse-v1.0.0.zip` below.
2. Extract the `Smokehouse` folder into your RimWorld `Mods` folder.
3. Enable it in the mod list and restart.

## Compatibility

- RimWorld **1.6** (main) and **1.7** (unstable)
- No DLC required
- Pure XML — no C#, no Harmony, no vanilla defs patched
- Safe to add to an existing save
